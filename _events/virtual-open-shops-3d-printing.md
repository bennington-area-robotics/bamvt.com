Join Bennington Area Makers on Thursday evenings at 6:00 pm to learn about what your neighbors are making and share projects that you are working on. This is a time to informally share what you are doing, ask questions, and get new ideas for projects. To register for Thursday Evenings at BAM, register at <https://forms.gle/1xnVUSBw271gkuYc6>.

A Zoom meeting link will be sent to registrants the day of the meeting.
