Join Meridy Capella from [Evergreen Alterations](https://www.evergreenalterations.com/) to discuss Sewing Machine Troubleshooting and Q&A. With so many people rallying to the cause of mask making in response to the COVID-19 pandemic, we've been hearing the groans of bunched thread on a bobbin and seams that just don't seem right.

Meridy will cover threading, tension, bobbin Issues and other topics.

We will focus on two popular face mask patterns:

Deaconess Mask - <https://www.deaconess.com/SpecialPages/How-to-make-a-Face-Mask>

Fu Mask - <https://freesewing.org/blog/facemask-frenzy/>

We will also be joined by Amy Anselmo of the [Green Mountain Mask Makers](http://www.averywoodmd.net/green-mountain-mask-makers.html) who will help you get connected to this wonderful grassroots effort to make masks and get them in the hands of those who need them.
