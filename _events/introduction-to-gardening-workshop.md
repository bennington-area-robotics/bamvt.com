Beneath that snow and ice the soil is just waiting to help you grow food this summer. Join the [Bennington Area Makers](http://www.bamvt.org/) (BAM) and the [Bennington Chapter of UVM Extension Master Gardeners](https://www.facebook.com/Bennington-Chapter-UVM-Extension-Master-Gardeners-142803249110599/) for this workshop that will explore the basics of vegetable gardening.  This workshop is free, but we are requesting attendees to register to that we can ensure we have enough supplies and help. ## About Bennington Master Gardeners

The Bennington Chapter of UVM Master Gardeners is a local group of volunteer gardening leaders who partner with local community groups to create access to plants and green space for health and well-being, to grow and distribute local food, to create resilient landscapes, to promote invasive species management, to educate on ways to help wildlife/pollinators, and to teach their communities how to compost and maintain healthy soil ecology. For more information on the [Bennington Chapter of UVM Master Gardeners](https://www.uvm.edu/extension/mastergardener), please [visit their facebook page](http://go.uvm.edu/bennmastergardeners).
