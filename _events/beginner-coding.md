Youth ages 7+ are invited to come explore computer science through coding. Hands-on puzzles and games start the adventure in the physical world, then we'll move into [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) and try out [Makey-Makey](https://makeymakey.com/)! McCullough Library, 4-H and BAM are teaming up for this geeky-fun Saturday workshop. Bring a brown bag lunch to re-fuel midway through. No experience necessary. Come check it out!
