# Support BAM

BAM is 100% volunteer-run and our current activities focus heavily on offering youth robotics as a learning and development experience.

We welcome offers of support in any form and are always in need of volunteers and financial support.

If you'd like to volunteer email info@bamvt.org. The only requirements are that you care about kids and their communities, you pass a youth protection screening, and attend a youth protection course. You do not need to have any specific technical skill or background.

If you'd like to make a financial donation you can do so online using [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/donate/?hosted_button_id=HPQY5NA3Z59C2) or mail a check made payable to Bennington Area Makers to PO Box 4332, Bennington, VT 05201.
